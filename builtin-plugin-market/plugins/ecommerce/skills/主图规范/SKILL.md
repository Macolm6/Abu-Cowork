# Main image rules

The main images decide whether a shopper clicks in the search results and whether they keep looking. Plan them as a set in which every image has one job.

## Default set of five

1. **Image 1, the click image**: the product alone and recognisable at thumbnail size, filling most of the frame, on a clean background. Follow the platform's rules for the first image (some platforms or categories require a white background or forbid text and logos on it).
2. **Image 2, the main benefit**: the strongest selling point, shown rather than told, with at most one short line of text.
3. **Image 3, the proof or detail**: a close-up of material, structure or the feature that supports the benefit.
4. **Image 4, the usage scene**: the product in real use, at a size the shopper can relate to.
5. **Image 5, specifications or what you get**: size, contents of the box, or the bundle for each SKU.

Add a white-background image or a vertical (3:4) image when the platform uses them for recommendations or campaigns, and a main video when the platform offers the slot. SKU images must show exactly the option they name.

## Rules for every image

- The product's colour, shape, proportions, material and packaging match the real goods. Never show accessories, effects or features the product does not have.
- Text is short, large and readable on a phone; no absolute or unverifiable claims; no other brands' logos or names.
- Use only photos, fonts and materials the store has rights to.
- Keep the visual style consistent across the set.

## Platform format check

Size, aspect ratio, file size, background, text and logo rules, and the number of image and video slots differ by platform (Taobao/Tmall, JD, Pinduoduo, Douyin, Xiaohongshu) and by category, and they change over time. State the requirements you are applying for the user's platform and category, and add "以平台当前的图片规范为准".

## Output

A table: image number, job, content, composition and angle, text overlay, notes for the photographer or image tool. When reviewing existing images, give the same table with a verdict and a concrete fix for each image. If an image-generation tool is used, describe the product with the same exact attributes in every prompt, and say that generated images must be checked against the real product before publishing.
