# Detail page structure

Build the page from the product information the user supplies. Every factual claim (material, size, capacity, effect, certification, warranty, shipping) must come from that information; where something is missing, write a placeholder such as `[质保期限]` and list all placeholders at the end. Do not invent test data, sales numbers, awards or endorsements.

Customers read detail pages on a phone and stop early. Put what decides the purchase first, keep each screen to one idea, and keep text short enough to read on an image.

## Screens

1. **First screen**: product name, the single strongest benefit in one line, and the one fact that proves it. This screen must make the customer want to keep scrolling.
2. **Core selling points** (three to five): each one as a short heading (the benefit), one or two lines of explanation (the supporting fact), and the image that proves it. Order them by what the user's reviews, questions or competitor gaps show customers care about most.
3. **Usage scenes**: who uses it, where and how. Show the product in real use.
4. **Details and craftsmanship**: close-ups that support quality claims made above.
5. **Specifications**: a clear table of the user's specifications; add a size guide, compatibility list or what is in the box when relevant.
6. **Comparison** (optional): versus the customer's alternative or the previous version, using only facts the user supplied. Never name or disparage a competitor brand.
7. **Answers to objections**: the three to five questions or worries that stop people buying, taken from the user's chat logs, Q&A or negative reviews when available, each with a short answer.
8. **After-sales and trust**: shipping, returns, warranty and service exactly as the store's policy states.

## Platform notes

Taobao/Tmall and JD pages are long image-led pages; Pinduoduo customers are price-sensitive, so put the value and the bundle contents early; Douyin and Xiaohongshu shoppers often arrive from content, so the first screens should match the video or note that brought them. Image width, length limits and text rules differ by platform and change; tell the user to confirm the current image guidelines.

## Output

For each screen: purpose, headline, body copy, image brief (what to show, composition, text overlay). Then a compliance check of all copy for absolute and unverifiable claims with alternatives, and the list of placeholders still to fill.
